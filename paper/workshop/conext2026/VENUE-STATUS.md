# Venue Status: CoNEXT 2026 Workshop Path

Checked: 2026-06-11.

## Current Official Facts

CoNEXT 2026 is in Utrecht, The Netherlands, December 7-10, 2026. The official
site describes the conference as the 22nd International Conference on emerging
Networking EXperiments and Technologies.

The main CoNEXT/PACMNET CFP is live, but both main paper deadlines relevant to
the 2026 conference have passed for a new submission:

- December cycle: registration December 5, 2025; submission December 12, 2025.
- June cycle: registration May 29, 2026; submission June 5, 2026.

The main submission instructions allow long papers with up to 16 pages of main
content plus references and up to four appendix pages, and short papers with up
to 10 pages plus references and up to two appendix pages. Submissions are
double-anonymous.

The workshop-proposal CFP is live, but its proposal deadline has also passed.
It says selected workshops were scheduled to be announced June 8, 2026, workshop
CFPs before June 15, 2026, workshop abstract registration July 7, 2026, and
workshop paper submission July 14, 2026. It also says workshop papers, unless
opted out, will be published in the same proceedings as conference short papers,
not in the PACMNET journal issue.

The artifact-evaluation page is live. For June-submission papers, the artifact
evaluation window is October 28, 2026 to November 18, 2026. The Entmoot private
five-day artifacts are not ready for public artifact badging without a separate
sanitization and release decision.

## Implication For Entmoot

The W6 package is ready as a handoff for a CoNEXT-style decentralization
workshop, but it should not claim a specific CoNEXT 2026 DIN workshop until the
accepted 2026 workshop page/CFP exists publicly. The verified precedent remains
the CoNEXT 2024 DIN workshop, which IETF described as a forum for research and
discussion on Internet decentralization, with participants from networking,
law/policy, and digital-rights communities.

The safest submission target once a suitable workshop CFP is live is a
workshop/short-paper format. If the workshop CFP does not define its own page
limit, start from the CoNEXT short-paper envelope: 10 pages of main content,
references outside the limit, and up to two appendix pages.
